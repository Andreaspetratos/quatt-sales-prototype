Underfloor mixing manifold

A mixing valve on the underfloor heating reduces water temperature. With a heat pump we want the manifold to run at the same low temperature as the rest of the system — so the mixing valve usually has to go.

Checklist with the customer

  • Is there a separate mixing manifold for the underfloor circuit?
  • Does it have a thermostatic head or a fixed-temp valve?
  • Is the manifold accessible (for the installer to rework or bypass)?
  • Is the underfloor heating the only emitter on its level, or mixed with radiators?

Action options

  • Bypass already in place: nothing extra needed.
  • Mixing valve present: Quatt will adjust on installation day (no extra charge if accessible).
  • No floor heating: skip this section.

Electricity requirements

Check the meter cabinet (meterkast) to confirm enough free capacity and modules.

Hybrid Single

  • 1-phase, 1 × 16 A dedicated group
  • 2 free modules in the meterkast

Hybrid Duo / All-E Medium

  • 1-phase or 3-phase, 1 × 16 A dedicated group
  • 3 free modules in the meterkast

All-E Large / Extra Large

  • 3-phase, 1 × 16 A dedicated group recommended
  • 4 free modules in the meterkast
  • Check for porcelain groups → switchboard upgrade required

Common upgrades needed

  • Werkschakelaar (work switch) installation incl. wiring — €299
  • Groepenkast aanpassing — €349 (or €649 with werkschakelaar)
  • Groepenkast vervanging L1 — €950 / €1,249 with werkschakelaar
  • Groepenkast vervanging L2 — €1,250 / €1,549 with werkschakelaar

Piping route — outside

From the outdoor unit to the wall penetration. Length, color of covers, and visibility matter.

Things to check

  • Distance from outdoor unit to the boiler / utility room
  • Cover color preference: grey or off-white
  • Penetration points: gevel, kozijn, or kruipruimte
  • Visible vs. concealed sections

Piping route — inside

From the wall entry point to the CV connection / boiler.

Things to check

  • Crawlspace accessibility & height (≥ 60 cm dry recommended)
  • CV pipe material (copper / steel) and diameter (22 / 28 mm)
  • Number of drilling holes (concrete vs. wood)
  • Existing routes that can be reused

HomeBattery placement

Battery should be wall-mounted or floor-standing in a dry, frost-free space.

Required conditions

  • Frost-free (≥ 5°C year round)
  • Ventilation: ≥ 10 cm free space around the unit
  • Wall load capacity: ≥ 60 kg per module
  • Distance to meterkast: as short as practical (≤ 15 m preferred)

Cable route to the switchboard

The battery connects to a dedicated group in the meterkast.

Things to check

  • Route length and accessible path (kruipruimte, leidingschacht, plafond)
  • Drilling: number of holes through walls, floors, ceilings
  • Cable type: 5 × 6 mm² for typical battery installations

Switchboard (meterkast)

A dedicated 3-phase group is required for the HomeBattery.

Check

  • Phase type (1-phase / 3-phase) — battery requires 3-phase
  • Free modules: ≥ 4
  • Main fuse: ≥ 3 × 25 A recommended
  • Porcelain groups → switchboard replacement required

Chill placement

Quatt Chill is added to the existing heat pump system. It needs space near the outdoor unit and indoor connections to the radiators.

Things to check

  • Space near the existing Quatt outdoor unit
  • Path from outdoor unit to indoor wall
  • Color preference: Calm Green or Soft Sand

Radiator pipes (Chill)

Chill reuses the existing radiator infrastructure. Check that pipes are insulated where condensation could occur.

Things to check

  • Radiator pipe insulation (visible runs)
  • Condensate drainage near radiators (drip trays / sleeves)
  • Floor / wall finishes near radiator brackets

Meerwerk (extra werk)

Anything that goes beyond the standard installation: relocating the connection point, renovation work around the installation, or staircase access (Verplaatsingskosten L2/L3).

When to add

  • Verplaatsingskosten L2 (€390): floor with stairs without a clear path.
  • Verplaatsingskosten L3 (€500): 2+ floors of staircase access.
  • Kleine relocatie aansluitpunt (€749): small move of the connection point.
  • Grote relocatie aansluitpunt (€2,000): larger reroute.
  • Renovatie en projectmanagement (€3,500): coordination with other trades during renovation.
